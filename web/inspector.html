20XX Rust engine inspector

Melee 1.02 game logic, hand-ported to Rust and running here as WebAssembly. This is not the playable game yet: it loads data from your own disc and runs the parts of the engine that are ported so far.

1 Open your disc

Choose a Super Smash Bros. Melee NTSC 1.02 ISO (vanilla or 20XX 5.0.2). It is read locally with File.slice: nothing is uploaded.

2 Fighter data

Attributes from the fighter's Pl??.dat, read with exactly the bits on your disc.

All ftCo_DatAttrs fields
Fighter-specific attributes (ftData::ext_attr)

3 Air physics lab

The ported Fall physics (ft_80084DB0: fast-fall check, gravity, air drift), integrated like Fighter_procUpdate with no knockback or wind. Starts at rest in the air. Input passes through the ported HSD pad library and Fighter_procInput.

Source: a hand port of the Melee decompilation. No game data is hosted here; everything comes from the disc you open. Super Smash Bros. Melee is © Nintendo / HAL Laboratory.
